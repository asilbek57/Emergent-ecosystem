# Emergent-ecosystem
A Python simulation where cells evolve speed and vision under different food scarcity
The aim of it is to find out how food scarcity affects different sizes of population. It is inspired by evolution concepts in biology and is very simplified.

For the start, this simulation has two classes: 
Cell : Individual organisms that track x , y positions. Their color is green that gradually blacks out as their energy drops down.
World : designed to hold cells and operate an entire group of them. For visualising them, pygame was used. 
Genome : Holds data for generations to inherit
